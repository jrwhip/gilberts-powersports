Common Questions

Frequently Asked

Everything you need to know before you ride.

Do you offer financing?
Yes. We work with several lenders to offer competitive rates on all new and used vehicles. Stop by or call us to get pre-approved. Approval is typically same-day.
Can I schedule a test ride?
Absolutely. We encourage test rides on every vehicle we carry. Give us a call or stop by during business hours and we'll get you behind the wheel on actual terrain, not just a parking lot.
Do you accept trade-ins?
We do. Bring your current machine in and we'll give you a fair market appraisal on the spot. We accept ATVs, UTVs, and motorcycles from all brands.
What brands do you service?
While we specialize in Segway Powersports, our technicians are experienced with most major brands. Contact our service department for specific questions about your machine.
Where are you located?
We're based in Utah and serve riders across the state and beyond. Check our contact section for the full address, hours, and directions.
Do you ship vehicles?
We can arrange shipping for customers outside our immediate area. Costs vary by distance. Reach out and we'll get you a quote along with an estimated delivery window.
